# Development fixture policy

The bundled pickup artwork is original, synthetic test material created for the deterministic adapter. It is not an approved Toyota Hilux reference, must not be published as customer-facing vehicle art, and cannot satisfy production identity approval.

A production job must replace it with a commercially approved reference set, matching provenance and SHA-256 records, `developmentMode: false`, live-provider credentials, human approvals, and a production master-video deliverable.
