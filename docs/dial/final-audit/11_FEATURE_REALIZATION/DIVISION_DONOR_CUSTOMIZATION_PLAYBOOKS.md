# Division Donor Customization Playbooks

## Spare

**Base experience:** Mercur marketplace interaction architecture.  
**Visual polish:** Nimara / Your Next Store quality bar.  
**Fitment:** SandPIM/ACES-PIES as schema reference.  
**Specialist extension:** DIAL EPC + hero/CGI/exploded generator.

Preserve marketplace browse/offer/checkout clarity. Replace donor auth/catalogue/order/payment state. The DIAL customer experience becomes vehicle-first: Garage context, visual/EPC/search entry modes, fitment evidence, supplier freshness, delivery, return/claim and Support OS.

## Tech

**Primary:** `Sachinrajawat/FixItNow` MIT PORT-WHOLESALE.  
**Secondary:** NearServe/Homezy UX comparison.  
**Slots:** Cal.com where suitable.

Keep useful FixItNow service discovery, provider/service presentation and booking page structure. Replace donor auth/persistence/payments/job state. Add DIAL Trade/JobClass, direct booking + Opportunity Marketplace, Job Reserve, diagnosis/quote/variation, HIRA/PPE/LOTO, evidence, WHT, RCE and provider quality. Retokenize everything into DIAL rather than shipping a visibly separate FixItNow app.

## Groceries

Instacart and local Zimbabwe retail are behavioral references, not code authorities. Build DIAL-native branch modules around substitution control, weighted goods, picking transparency, Pantry/scheduled baskets and merchant/shopper modes. Reuse DIAL design system and shared Activity/Support.

## Laundry

Laundryheap/Rinse inform consumer booking/status; CleanCloud/Cents inform facility operations. DIAL owns the actual custody/intake/production/QC state. The customer UI stays simple while the backend/operator flows retain full chain-of-custody rigor.

## Fleet / Vehicle Hub

LubeLogger is the strongest maintenance/history UX/schema donor. Use it to accelerate service history, reminders and cost views, but DIAL Vehicle Hub identity and shared Tech/Spare/Assist history remain authoritative. OpenRemote may provide telemetry observations only.

## Care

DIAL-native. Design around clear entitlement/billing/benefit state rather than cloning a generic subscription SaaS UI. Deep integration with Vehicle Hub, Tech, Assist and Spare is more important than a donor.

## Assist

DIAL-native safety-first flow on top of shared Delivery/maps/dispatch primitives. Minimize interaction before dispatch; surface human help early. Do not transplant food-delivery UI literally.

## Projects

OpenProject provides work-package/dependency/Gantt/meeting UX concepts. DIAL client experience must additionally support assessment, quotation, progressive funding, materials via Spare, teams via Tech, evidence/safety and client approvals/claims.

## Dial Health

The specialist ZHOTN master and qualified health donor corpus remain authoritative. Main DIAL adds only shared identity/payment/delivery/support/Command Centre contracts where lawful. Do not visually force health into a retail branch skin; it has a dedicated app family using the DIAL design language at a more clinical/trust-focused density.

## Corporate

Frappe HR, ERPNext, InvenTree, Snipe-IT, GLPI, Documenso, Eramba and OpenProject contribute workflows, algorithms or UX according to the locked Corporate assimilation matrix. All are recomposed into the DIAL Command Design System and one DIAL Identity/Ledger/Evidence architecture.

## Customer Service

Chatwoot provides human support inbox/help-center infrastructure. DIAL Support Agent is the automated layer using the DIAL AI gateway. Intercom/Fin patterns are reference only for role specialization and human handoff. No support product decides refunds, liability or clinical outcomes.

## Command Centre

Studio Admin establishes the visual shell quality bar; shadcn/ui provides owned primitives; Tremor Raw provides selected data components; Refine provides headless repetitive-resource plumbing. Every page remains a DIAL control-room experience, not generic SaaS CRUD.

### v1.6 Corporate Finance / People details

For Corporate Finance:
- ERPNext is a schema/workflow/algorithm reference for mature accounting/payment-entry/journal behavior;
- do **not** port ERPNext's ledger as a second financial authority;
- DIAL Ledger owns every posting;
- DIAL Command Design System owns the bank-statement UI.

For People:
- Frappe HR informs Employee/Position/onboarding/workforce behaviors;
- DIAL extends Position into a stronger first-class contract/permission/financial-authority object;
- Documenso remains the e-sign ceremony service;
- DIAL Records owns the signed contract/evidence state.

Payment providers are `EXTERNAL_ADAPTER`s, never donors of DIAL accounting authority:
EcoCash Direct, Paynow, ContiPay and PayPal.
