# Division-Specific Customer Support Playbooks

## SPARE

**Support scope:** Fitment/part identification, offer/order, delivery, returns/warranty, counterfeit

**Automated safe capabilities:**
- order/offer status
- fitment explanation from approved evidence
- return-window explanation
- create fitment review/return/support case

**Mandatory human/specialist escalation:**
- counterfeit allegation
- property/engine damage attributed to wrong part
- high-value fitment dispute
- conflicting evidence

## TECH

**Support scope:** Booking, technician arrival, quote/variation, safety, workmanship

**Automated safe capabilities:**
- booking/job status
- reschedule if allowed
- explain quote state
- collect variation question/evidence
- create no-show/workmanship case

**Mandatory human/specialist escalation:**
- injury/safety
- property damage
- workmanship liability
- technician conduct/fraud
- large variation dispute

## GROCERIES

**Support scope:** Picking, substitutions, final weight/price, delivery, missing/spoiled items

**Automated safe capabilities:**
- pick/delivery status
- substitution approve/reject
- report missing/damaged/spoiled item
- show recall guidance

**Mandatory human/specialist escalation:**
- food safety/cold-chain risk
- large/repeated loss
- restricted product
- conflicting delivery evidence

## LAUNDRY

**Support scope:** Pickup/custody, inspection/final price, production, lost/damaged garments

**Automated safe capabilities:**
- custody/status

- collect photos

**Mandatory human/specialist escalation:**
- high-value garment loss
- property/damage dispute
- healthcare contamination
- conflicting custody evidence

## FLEET

**Mandatory human/specialist escalation:**
