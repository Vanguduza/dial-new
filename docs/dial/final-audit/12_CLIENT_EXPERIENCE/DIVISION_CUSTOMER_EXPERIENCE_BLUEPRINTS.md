# Division Customer Experience Blueprints

## Spare — DIAL Consumer / Web / WhatsApp

**Enter:** Home shortcut, Garage/Vehicle Hub, search, saved vehicle, campaign/deep link.  
**Core:** vehicle → hero/search/EPC/category → fitment → part/offer → cart → payment → fulfilment.  
**In-flight:** supplier confirmation, pickup/delivery, substitution/failover where policy allows.  
**Recovery:** wrong/damaged/missing/counterfeit/fitment/late supplier/delivery issue.  
**Complete:** receipt, fitment/order history, warranty/return window, Vehicle Hub update, support/claim.

## Tech

**Enter:** service/trade browse, vehicle/property context, guided problem intake, repeat provider/job.  
**Core:** classify → direct booking or Opportunity Marketplace → technician/proposal → diagnosis/quote → reserve → scheduled job.  
**In-flight:** technician ETA, safety prerequisites, check-in, evidence, variations, team status.  
**Recovery:** no-show, unsafe condition, scope dispute, property damage, workmanship issue, offline tech.  
**Complete:** customer confirmation, evidence pack, invoice/receipt, rating, claim/rework window.

## Groceries

**Enter:** store, search, favorites, Pantry, scheduled basket.  
**Core:** browse → cart → substitution preferences → slot → estimate/ceiling → order.  
**In-flight:** merchant/shopper picking, weighted finalization, substitution decisions, delivery/pickup.  
**Recovery:** short pick, no substitute, spoiled/damaged/missing/wrong item, delay, cold-chain/recall.  
**Complete:** final receipt, Pantry update, reorder, issue/remedy tracking.

## Laundry

**Enter:** service/plan, repeat booking, scheduled pickup.  
**Core:** estimate/max → pickup → custody → facility inspection/itemization → final price approval → processing/QC → return.  
**Recovery:** count mismatch, pre-existing damage dispute, lost garment, damage/shrink/color bleed, machine/facility delay, failed delivery.  
**Complete:** item/custody history, final receipt, claim window, subscription next pickup.

## Vehicle Hub

**Enter:** Garage.  
**Core:** vehicle identity/docs/mileage/history → reminders → branch shortcuts.  
**Recovery:** duplicate/conflicting vehicle, wrong history linkage, ownership transfer, document issue.  
**Complete:** trusted longitudinal vehicle record.

## Care

**Enter:** eligible vehicle/profile, plan page, Vehicle Hub.  
**Core:** eligibility → subscribe → billing → entitlements → invoke Tech/Assist/Spare benefit.  
**Recovery:** failed renewal, entitlement dispute, benefit unavailable, grace/suspension.  
**Complete:** entitlement consumption/history and renewal state.

## Assist

**Enter:** persistent high-visibility Assist action, Care benefit, Vehicle Hub, incident deep link.  
**Core:** safety → location → coverage → provider → dispatch → ETA → arrival/service/tow → proof.  
**Recovery:** unsafe location, no provider, provider cancels/no-show, routing outage, failed repair, tow damage.  
**Complete:** incident history, receipt/entitlement, claim/support.

## Projects

**Enter:** project request, prior assessment, business workspace.  
**Core:** request → assessment → scope/BoQ/quote → funding → project/milestones.  
**In-flight:** dependencies, materials, progress evidence, variations, approvals.  
**Recovery:** delay, safety stop, cost overrun, milestone rejection, material failure, site/property damage.  
**Complete:** handover pack, final financial state, defects/claim period.

## Fleet — DIAL Business

**Enter:** organization dashboard.  
**Core:** fleet → vehicle → defect/maintenance/compliance → Tech/Spare execution.  
**Recovery:** downtime, missing provider, repeated defect, document expiry, telemetry outage.  
**Complete:** cost, history, compliance and availability.

## Dial Health — standalone

The detailed health specialist master remains authoritative.

Client experience must still guarantee:
- authenticated patient/family/delegated context;
- appointments/referrals;
- prescription/medicine/pharmacy visibility;
- claims/payment status;
- medicine fulfilment/delivery;
- emergency entry;
- health support with strict safe escalation;
- consent/access/history.

The Health app must not use the general consumer support agent for clinical advice.
