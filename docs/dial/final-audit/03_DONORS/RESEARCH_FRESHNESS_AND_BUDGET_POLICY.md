# Research Freshness & Budget Policy

Every research record stores subject/module, sources, verified_at, revision/hash, confidence, criticality, review_after, refresh_triggers, findings path and affected Feature IDs.

Refresh only for a material trigger: implementation reaches unmapped behavior, donor revision/security changes, time-sensitive rule ages out, tests reveal unknown behavior, regulation/provider API/licence changes.

Default budget:
- ordinary feature: 1 primary + up to 2 secondary sources + consequential official standard;
- strategic money/safety/compliance/catalogue: QDRS depth allowed, critical unknowns zero;
- max 2 strategic deep QDRS studies active concurrently;
- max 4 ordinary donor qualification studies active;
- every research task names the implementation decision it unlocks.

Do not rerun research simply because a session/context restarted.
