# Dial Health / ZHOTN — Standalone Division Decision

The earlier audit classification `SPECIALIST_VERTICAL_PENDING_PORTFOLIO_LOCK` is **superseded**.

## Locked status

**Dial Health / ZHOTN is a required standalone DIAL business division.**

It contains its own specialist product family and health operating network, including the existing authoritative architecture for:

- My Health;
- Practice OS;
- Pharmacy OS;
- Hospital OS;
- Transaction Network;
- Pharma Cloud;
- Emergency Network;
- Communication Gateway;
- Developer Platform.

## Architectural relationship to Main DIAL

Dial Health is neither:
- a submodule of Corporate Management;
- a generic extension of Dial a Tech;
- nor an optional future portfolio experiment.

It is a peer DIAL division with a specialist bounded architecture.

It may reuse shared DIAL horizontal capabilities only through explicit contracts, for example:
- Party/Identity where compatible;
- Money/Payments/Ledger rails where legally and clinically appropriate;
- Delivery;
- Procurement/Inventory primitives;
- Communications;
- Evidence/Audit;
- Command Centre development and infrastructure telemetry.

Health-specific clinical, pharmacy, funder, emergency, consent, interoperability, regulatory and safety authorities remain owned by Dial Health/ZHOTN.

## Certification

Ordinary DIAL integration green does not substitute for healthcare-specific safety/regulatory certification.

## Command Centre

`requiredControlRooms` must include a **Dial Health** division room with:
- market/network;
- patients/customers;
- providers/facilities;
- pharmacy/medicine operations;
- transactions/claims;
- emergency-network state;
- safety/compliance;
- money;
- development;
- infrastructure;
- AI;
- audit/evidence;
- controls.
